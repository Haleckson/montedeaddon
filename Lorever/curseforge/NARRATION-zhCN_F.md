## Summary (English)

A woman's storyteller voice for Lorever: every lore page, read aloud in natural Chinese (Simplified). Optional add-on, needs Lorever.

## Description (English)

Lorever Narration (Chinese Female)

A natural Chinese (Simplified) storyteller voice for the Lorever narrator, read by a woman: the "Xiaoxiao" voice.

Lorever already reads its pages aloud with the game's own text to speech. This optional pack replaces that robotic voice with a calm, natural Chinese (Simplified) storyteller. Every region, figure, people, event and writing page in Lorever has already been narrated, so there is nothing to set up and nothing else to run.

How it works

1. Install Lorever and this pack.
2. Open any Lorever page and press the play button of the narrator.
3. That is all. The narrator uses the pack by itself.

Two voices

This is the woman's voice for Chinese (Simplified). There is also a man's voice: Lorever Narration (Chinese Male). You can install both. Choose one under "Pack" in the narrator panel (/lorever narrator).

Good to know

- Lorever is required (version 1.8.0 or newer). The CurseForge app installs it for you.
- The pack is used when Lorever shows its content in Chinese (Simplified). To switch the language: /lorever language zh
- In the narrator panel (/lorever narrator) you can choose the voice: Automatic, the game's built-in voice, or a voice pack. Automatic picks your pack when one is installed.
- If a line has no recording (for example, after a Lorever update adds new text), your other pack or the game's own voice reads that line. Nothing breaks.
- No program runs outside the game. The pack is only sound files and a small index.
- Fully compatible with Questie.

What is inside

- The pack holds only Lorever's own text, read aloud. It contains no audio, text or art from Blizzard Entertainment, and no text from the game's books.
- The files are Ogg Vorbis audio, kept small to save disk space.

AI disclaimer

The voice in this pack was generated by a text to speech model, not recorded by a person. The recordings were made on the author's own computer with Kokoro-82M, an open model released under the Apache 2.0 license, using its "Xiaoxiao" voice at 1.30 speed. The text that is read is Lorever's own lore, written for the add-on. No online AI service was used, and no real person's voice was cloned or imitated.

Other languages

Each Lorever language has its own separate, optional packs, with a man's voice and a woman's voice: English, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Español, 简体中文 and Русский.

## Native text (optional, below the English one)

Summary: 为 Lorever 带来女声说书人：每一页传说，都用自然的中文朗读。可选插件，需要 Lorever。

Lorever Narration (Chinese Female)

为 Lorever 的讲述者带来自然的中文女声说书人："Xiaoxiao"。

Lorever 本来使用游戏自带的文字转语音来朗读页面。这个可选语音包用一位沉稳自然的中文女声说书人取代那种机械的声音。Lorever 中的每个地区、人物、民族、事件和文献页面都已朗读完毕，无需任何设置，也不需要运行其他程序。

使用方法

1. 安装 Lorever 和这个语音包。
2. 打开任意 Lorever 页面，按下讲述者的播放按钮。
3. 就这样。讲述者会自动使用这个语音包。

两种声音

这是中文女声。另有一个男声：Lorever Narration (Chinese Male)。两个都可以安装。在讲述者面板（/lorever narrator）的"Pack"中选择其中一个。

须知

- 需要 Lorever（1.8.0 或更新版本）。CurseForge 应用会为你安装。
- 当 Lorever 以中文显示内容时使用这个语音包（/lorever language zh）。
- 在讲述者面板（/lorever narrator）中可以选择声音：自动、游戏自带的声音或语音包。安装了语音包时，"自动"会使用它。
- 如果某一句没有录音（例如 Lorever 更新后加入了新文字），会由你的另一个语音包或游戏自带的声音朗读。不会出错。
- 没有任何程序在游戏之外运行。语音包只有声音文件和一个小索引。
- 与 Questie 完全兼容。

包含内容

- 语音包只包含 Lorever 自己的文字的朗读。不包含暴雪娱乐的任何音频、文字或美术，也不包含游戏中书籍的文字。
- 文件为 Ogg Vorbis 音频，体积小，节省磁盘空间。

AI 声明

这个语音包中的声音由文字转语音模型生成，不是真人录音。录音在作者自己的电脑上用 Kokoro-82M（Apache 2.0 许可的开放模型）的"Xiaoxiao"声音以 1.30 倍速生成。朗读的文字是为本插件撰写的 Lorever 自己的传说。没有使用任何在线 AI 服务，也没有克隆或模仿任何真人的声音。
