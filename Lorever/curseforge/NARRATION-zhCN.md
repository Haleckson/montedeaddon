## Description (English)

Lorever Narration (简体中文)

A natural Chinese (Simplified) storyteller voice for the Lorever narrator.

Lorever already reads its pages aloud with the game's own text to speech. This optional pack replaces that robotic voice with a calm, natural Chinese (Simplified) storyteller. Every region, figure, people, event, book and letter in Lorever has already been narrated, so there is nothing to set up and nothing else to run.

How it works

1. Install Lorever and this pack.
2. Open any Lorever page and press the play button of the narrator.
3. That is all. The narrator uses the pack by itself.

Good to know

- Lorever is required (version 1.7.0 or newer). The CurseForge app installs it for you.
- The pack is used when Lorever shows its content in Chinese (Simplified). To switch the language: /lorever language zh
- In the narrator panel (/lorever narrator) you can choose the voice: Automatic, the game's built-in voice, or this voice pack. Automatic picks this pack when it is installed.
- If a line has no recording (for example, after a Lorever update adds new text), the game's own voice reads that line. Nothing breaks.
- No program runs outside the game. The pack is only sound files and a small index.
- Fully compatible with Questie.

What is inside

- The pack holds only Lorever's own text, read aloud. It contains no audio, text or art from Blizzard Entertainment, and no text from the game's books.
- The files are Ogg Vorbis audio, kept small to save disk space.

AI disclaimer

The voice in this pack was generated by a text to speech model, not recorded by a person. The recordings were made on the author's own computer with Kokoro-82M, an open model released under the Apache 2.0 license, using its "Yunxi" voice at 1.30 speed. The text that is read is Lorever's own lore, written for the add-on. No online AI service was used, and no real person's voice was cloned or imitated.

Other languages

Each Lorever language has its own separate, optional pack: English, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Español, 简体中文 and Русский.

## Native text (optional, below the English one)

Summary: 为 Lorever 带来说书人的声音：每一页传说、每一本书，都用自然的中文朗读。可选插件，需要 Lorever。

Lorever Narration (简体中文)

为 Lorever 的讲述者带来自然的说书人声音。

Lorever 本来使用游戏自带的文字转语音来朗读页面。这个可选语音包用一位沉稳自然的中文说书人取代那种机械的声音。Lorever 中的每个地区、人物、民族、事件、书籍和信件都已朗读完毕，无需任何设置，也不需要运行其他程序。

使用方法

1. 安装 Lorever 和本语音包。
2. 打开任意一页 Lorever，点击讲述者的播放按钮。
3. 就这样。讲述者会自动使用语音包。

须知

- 需要 Lorever（1.7.0 或更高版本）。CurseForge 应用会为你一并安装。
- 当 Lorever 以中文显示内容时使用本语音包（/lorever language zh）。
- 在讲述者面板（/lorever narrator）中可以选择声音：自动、游戏自带语音或本语音包。安装本语音包后，“自动”会使用它。
- 如果某句话没有录音（例如 Lorever 更新后加入了新文字），游戏自带的语音会朗读那一句，不会出错。
- 游戏之外不运行任何程序。语音包只包含音频文件和一个小索引。
- 与 Questie 完全兼容。

包含内容

- 语音包只包含 Lorever 自己的文字的朗读，不含暴雪娱乐的任何音频、文字或美术，也不含游戏内书籍的文字。

关于 AI 的说明

本语音包的声音由文字转语音模型生成，并非真人录制。音频在作者自己的电脑上用 Kokoro-82M（Apache 2.0 许可的开放模型）的“云希”声音以 1.30 倍速生成。朗读的文字是为本插件撰写的 Lorever 传说。未使用任何在线 AI 服务，也没有克隆或模仿任何真人的声音。
