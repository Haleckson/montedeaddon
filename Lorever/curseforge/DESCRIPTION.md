# 📜 Lorever — *the histories of Azeroth, uncovered as you travel*

> **An encyclopedia of Azeroth that writes itself as you play.**
> Every region, figure, people, war and relic has a page. Pages start in the fog and unlock as you explore, like quests.
> And a narrator can read them to you while you ride.

**Made for World of Warcraft: Forever** (client 1.60.1). 100% free. Fully compatible with **Questie**.

## 🗺️ Lore that unlocks as you play

- **Pages start locked.** Walk into a land, speak with a figure, finish the right quest: the page opens, with a banner and Lore experience.
- **Every name is a link.** Ink blue: a page you know. Faded blue: not yet discovered. Hover for a preview, click to read.
- **Figures have chapters.** Each chapter unlocks on its own, with mentions and **bonds**, the web of relations between the people of Azeroth.
- **Turquoise "!" marks** show undiscovered figures above their heads, on the world map and on the minimap.
- **The Lore tracker** lists what you track and the nearest undiscovered figures and writings, with distances.

## 📚 The library of Literature

- Every book, letter, journal, legend and inscription in the world, by region, with **where to find it**.
- **Missing**, **Read** and **Unlisted** lists, filters by kind, and search.
- Book icons on the map where a writing you have not read is waiting.
- When you read a writing in the world, its text is kept on your computer, so you can read it again inside Lorever.

## 🎙️ The narrator

Press **Listen** on any page. A small bar stays on screen with **pause**, **next section**, **back** and **stop**, so you can close the book and keep playing.

- A **queue** ("Listen next"), and it **remembers where it stopped**, even after `/reload`.
- **Travel mode:** on a flight, it reads the lands you fly over.
- **Pause in combat**, and read each new discovery aloud, if you like.

### 🎚️ The narrator panel — `/lorever narrator`

Choose **who reads each kind of page**, in each language:

| Kind of page | Default style |
| --- | --- |
| Regions and places | 📖 **Chronicler** — calm and clear, like an encyclopedia |
| Figures | 📖 **Chronicler** |
| Peoples, orders and events | ⚔️ **Epic** — deep and slow, for great events |
| Books and legends | 🕯️ **Storyteller** — warm and unhurried |
| Letters and journals | 🍃 **Serene** — light and gentle |

Click **Copy** to make your own profile: pick the voice, speed and volume. Every row has a **Test** button.

At the top of the panel you choose the voice: **Automatic**, the game's **built-in TTS**, or a **voice pack**.

## ⌨️ Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/lorever` | Open the book of lore |
| `/lorever narrator` | The narrator panel: voices, styles and speed |
| `/lorever listen` | Read the land you stand in |
| `/lorever pause` · `/lorever stop` | Pause or resume · stop the narrator |
| `/lorever level` | Your Lore and Literature levels |
| `/lorever nameplates` | Show friendly NPC nameplates, so lore marks can appear |
| `/lorever language auto\|en\|pt\|de\|fr\|es` | Change Lorever's language |
| `/lorever options` | Open the options |

Key bindings: **Options > Keybindings > AddOns > Lorever**. Minimap button: click for the book, right-click for the narrator.

## 🔒 Privacy and fair play

- Lorever **never** sends chat messages, never automates anything, and stays out of Blizzard's menus and panels.
- No account, no tracking. Nothing about you or your characters leaves your computer.
- Lorever ships **no Blizzard text, art or audio**. Book text is read from your own game, and stays on your computer.

## ❓ Questions

**Does it work with Questie?** Yes. Lorever is built to sit beside it, and can draw its marks with Questie's icons.
**Do I need a voice pack?** No. Without one, the narrator uses the game's own voice.
**Why can't I see the "!" above figures?** Turn on friendly NPC nameplates: `/lorever nameplates`.

## 🤖 AI Disclaimer

**The lore.** All of Lorever's lore was written with the help of AI. The AI worked only from facts found in the game itself and in Blizzard's official sources, and was told never to invent events, family ties or "lost chapters". The text is our own: no book, quest or dialogue text from the game is copied into the add-on. The translations (Português, Deutsch, Français, Español, 简体中文, Русский) were also made with AI, using each game client's official names for places, factions, races, classes and books. If you spot a mistake in the lore, please tell us in the comments and we will fix it.

**The narrator.** Lorever's narrator has two voices:

- **The game's own voice.** By default, Lorever reads with World of Warcraft's built-in text-to-speech. No AI is involved.
- **The voice packs (optional).** The narration in the Lorever Narration packs was generated with AI text-to-speech models, on the author's own computer: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) for English, Português, Español and 简体中文, and Piper voices (CC0) for Deutsch, Français and Русский. Only Lorever's own text is read. The packs hold no audio, text or art from Blizzard, and no text from the game's books. No online AI service was used, and no real person's voice was cloned or imitated.
