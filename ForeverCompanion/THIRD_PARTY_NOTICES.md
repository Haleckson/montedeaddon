## Wowhead: WoW Forever database (facts only)

`Data/Reference/Wowhead.lua` is generated by `Tools/import_wowhead.py` from
the public listings of Wowhead's WoW Forever database
(https://www.wowhead.com/forever): the title, level, faction and zone of
quests, and the name, level and zone of rares that WoW Forever added; and
from its tooltip service (`Tools/fetch_wowhead_names.py`): the name, and for
a few the place, of the creatures and objects that give quests. These are
facts about the game (the names are Blizzard's); no text, comment,
screenshot or guide of Wowhead is included. Wowhead is a trademark of its
owner; Forever Companion is not affiliated with it.

## Game-name dictionaries

`Tools/localization/build_content.py`: localized quest, NPC, item and object
names and NPC roles from the installed QuestieDB Forever 1.0.5 export, and
zone names from Questie's localization tables. Project sources:
https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB and https://github.com/Questie/Questie.

## Discovery lore and field notes

`Data/DiscoveryLoreData.lua` contains brief, independently worded summaries of
game facts, researched using Blizzard announcements, in-game quest information,
Wowhead NPC data, Warcraft Wiki and the other sources listed with each entry.
Each entry retains its source links, accessible through **Sources** in the journal.
The full articles and research caches are not distributed with the addon.
Research date: 11 October 2026. Where an individual backstory could not be
verified, the entry is labelled as field notes and describes known game facts.
Warcraft names, characters and setting belong to Blizzard Entertainment.
