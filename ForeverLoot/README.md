# ForeverLoot

A small loot browser for **WoW: Forever**. It shows you what drops in dungeons and raids, filtered down to the stuff your class and spec can actually use.

**Still in beta.** Loot tables for most dungeons and raids are not known yet. Empty rows and missing bosses will get filled in as those drops are figured out.

Type `/fl` in chat or click the minimap button to open it.

## What it does

**Shows all dungeon and raid loot in one window**
Two tabs: Dungeons and Raids. Every dungeon gets one row with its level range and all its drops as icons. For raids you pick a raid and see the loot boss by boss.

**Shows only relevant gear for the class and spec**
Pick your class, spec, slot and level range and the list only shows items that make sense for you. The addon looks at the actual item.

**All items**
Next to Favorites there is an **All items** button. Turn it on and the spec, weapon and stat filters are off, so you see everything your class can equip. Turn it off to get the filtered view back.

**Favorites**
Right-click any item to mark it as a favorite. Hit the **Favorites** button and the list shows only your favorites, so you can keep a personal "want" list.

**Normal item stuff works**
Hover for the tooltip, Shift-click to link it in chat, Ctrl-click to try it on in the dressing room. Anything you have equipped gets a green tick.

**Remembers your settings**
Your class, spec, filters, chosen tab and window position are saved. Escape closes the window. The minimap button can be dragged around or turned off (settings cog in the window, or in Interface Options).

## Commands

- `/fl` or `/foreverloot` – open or close the window
