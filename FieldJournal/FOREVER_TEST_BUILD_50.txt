Field Journal - Forever Test Build 50

Zephras Isle level 7-12 quest continuation.

Changes from Build 49:
- Added 63 additional Zephras Isle quest records.
- Added Shen'dar Highlands side quests and the Al'Aketh infiltration chain.
- Added the western pylon watchtower story and Shadowsong signet follow-up.
- Added Windfield Orchard / Gustberry Lowlands quests.
- Added Valanaar surveillance and faction-hub progression.
- Added High Order-specific and Windshaper-specific Valanaar story branches.
- Added the eastern pylon defense.
- Added the shared late Al'Aketh campaign through the Shrine of Akir.
- Added both faction versions of Confront Lorthuna, The Fate of Zephras, and What Comes Next.
- Added the Shadowgale / Nightclaw druid storyline through The Fate of the Den.
- Added The Fate of a Loved One at the Ruins of Ban'aethal.
- Added four verified Windshaper Skyborne Call of Fire quest IDs: 97243, 97244, 97245, 97257.
- Preserved all Build 47-49 additions.

Scope:
Build 50 carries the core Zephras Isle experience through level 12 and ends with
the faction-specific What Comes Next quests while the player is still on Zephras.
The level 13 travel/arrival chains into Dalaran/Stormwind and Mulgore/Orgrimmar
are intentionally reserved for the next transition pass.
